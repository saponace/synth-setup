[COMMENT]
Factory drum map from Arturia's manual section 9.10.5, laid out for
16-lane hapaxOS (3.10+). Page 1 (lanes 1-8) is the requested play set,
page 2 (9-16) the rest. Base notes 36-45; Colored variants = base+12
(notes 48-57; Cowbell 42 has no Color, so note 54 doesn't exist).
Two lane names are aliases -- the DrumBrute has no exact match: "Clap"
= Colored Snare 2 (note 50), "Crash" = Cymbal (note 41). Left out for
space (16-lane cap): Col Tom H (51), Col Tom L (52), Col FM (57).
Lane syntax is ROW:TRIG:CHAN:NOTENUMBER NAME; CHAN NULL inherits the
track channel (10).
[/COMMENT]
