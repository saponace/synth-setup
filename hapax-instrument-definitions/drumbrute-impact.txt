[COMMENT]
Factory drum map from Arturia's manual section 9.10.5: 10 base voices
(notes 36-45) plus Colored alt-sound variants (notes 48-57; Cowbell has
none). Hapax caps a definition at 16 drum lanes, so only 6 of the 9
Colored variants fit -- dropped Colored Tom H (51), Colored Tom L (52)
and Colored FM (57). Swap any of those in for a lane you don't use.
Lane syntax is ROW:TRIG:CHAN:NOTENUMBER NAME; CHAN NULL inherits the
track channel (5).
[/COMMENT]
