[COMMENT]
DFAM has no MIDI at all, so this holds Hapax track 2 as a gate-only
track. Gate 1 goes to the DFAM ADV/CLOCK jack: every note on this track
advances its onboard 8-step sequencer by one step. Pitch is ignored, only
the trigger matters, so step length and rate are what shape the part.
Untested on hardware, unlike the four MIDI definitions here.
[/COMMENT]
