[COMMENT]
Moog Subharmonicon. CC/CC_PAIR data from midi.guide (11 documented
parameters: VCO1/VCO2 frequency and their SUB1/SUB2 divider freqs as
14-bit MSB/LSB pairs, VCF/VCA envelope attack+decay as pairs, rhythm
generator logic mode, note priority). Note that incoming MIDI notes
separately transpose the onboard sequencer rather than playing 1:1,
and MIDI clock overrides the internal clock and relays out to DFAM
(see README.md notes) -- these CCs are direct parameter control, a
separate mechanism from that note-transpose behavior.
Routing set for this track: ch 2 via the Thru box (Hapax MIDI out A).
[/COMMENT]
