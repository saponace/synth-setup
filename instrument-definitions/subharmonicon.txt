[COMMENT]
Moog Subharmonicon has minimal MIDI: incoming notes transpose its own
onboard 4-step/polyrhythmic sequencer rather than playing 1:1, and
MIDI clock overrides its internal clock and relays out to DFAM (see
README.md notes). CC 91 sets Low/High/Last note priority -- the only
documented CC. Routing set for this track: ch 2 via the Thru box.
OUTPORT left NULL -- set it to whichever physical Hapax port (A/B/C/D)
feeds the Thru box once confirmed.
[/COMMENT]
